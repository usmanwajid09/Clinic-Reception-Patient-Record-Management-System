CLINIC RECEPTION & PATIENT RECORD MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
ROLES, FUNCTIONAL AUTHORITIES & PRIVACY BOUNDARIES
================================================================================
Course: Software Design & Analysis (SDA) - Project #2
Standard: Fixed Functional Requirements Specification (FR1 - FR30)
Security Model: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Principle of Least Privilege

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ACCESS CONTROL PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Clinic Reception & Patient Record Management System implements a strict
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) architecture. Users interact with the desktop
application exclusively according to their authenticated role.

In compliance with medical data privacy guidelines and the Principle of Least
Privilege, no user role is granted universal access:
- Clinicians (Doctors) cannot access front-desk billing or clinic revenue analytics.
- Receptionists cannot view confidential clinical diagnoses, consultation notes,
  or alter doctor-issued prescriptions.
- Administrators cannot diagnose patients, prescribe medications, or collect payments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ROLE DEFINITIONS, CAPABILITIES & BOUNDARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 1: RECEPTIONIST (Front Desk Operations & Cashier)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Authority Level:
  Front-Desk Staff Operational Authority.
  Responsible for patient onboarding, scheduling, queue management, and cashier billing.

Allowed Functional Requirements (FRs):
  * FR1  - Register a new patient (ID, name, DOB, gender, address, contact, emergency contact).
  * FR2  - Search an existing patient by patient ID, name, or contact number.
  * FR3  - Update permitted patient demographic and contact information.
  * FR4  - View patient visit history summary (dates, doctors visited, appointment status).
  * FR5  - Mandatory validation of patient information prior to registration.
  * FR9  - Mark a doctor as Available or Unavailable for a specified day.
  * FR10 - View list of doctors by department and specialization.
  * FR11 - Create an appointment for a registered patient with an available doctor.
  * FR12 - Record appointment date, time, sequential token number, and status.
  * FR13 - Enforce double-booking prevention engine (no overlapping doctor slots).
  * FR14 - Reschedule or cancel existing appointments.
  * FR15 - Maintain appointment lifecycle status (Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled, No-Show).
  * FR16 - View daily appointment queue for any doctor.
  * FR24 - Generate billing invoice for completed consultations (fee + fixed clinic charges).
  * FR25 - Record payment collection and payment status (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
  * FR26 - Generate and print receipt for paid bills via desktop printing engine.

Permitted Desktop Screens:
  1. Patient Records Desk (PatientManagementPanel)
  2. Doctor Directory & Duty Status (DoctorManagementPanel - Read & Status Only)
  3. Appointment Desk & Queue (AppointmentManagementPanel)
  4. Billing & Cashier Terminal (BillingManagementPanel)

Privacy & Forbidden Actions (STRICTLY PROHIBITED):
  [X] CANNOT access Doctor Clinical Workstation (DoctorConsultationPanel).
  [X] CANNOT read private clinical symptoms, medical diagnoses, or doctor's private notes.
  [X] CANNOT add, modify, or remove prescription medicines (FR18, FR19, FR20, FR21).
  [X] CANNOT register new doctors, modify doctor qualifications, or set consultation fees (Admin only).
  [X] CANNOT access clinic-wide revenue analytics or doctor financial reports (FR27 - FR30).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 2: DOCTOR (Medical Practitioner / Clinician)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Authority Level:
  Clinical Examination & Diagnostic Authority.
  Responsible for patient medical examinations, diagnosis, and prescribing medicines.

Allowed Functional Requirements (FRs):
  * FR7  - Associate with one medical department.
  * FR9  - View and toggle own daily availability (Available / Unavailable).
  * FR10 - View doctor directory and colleagues by department.
  * FR16 - View doctor's appointment queue for today and selected dates (OWN queue only).
  * FR17 - Open confidential medical record of patients scheduled with this doctor.
  * FR18 - Record clinical symptoms, medical diagnosis, and confidential clinical notes.
  * FR19 - Create electronic prescription for an active consultation.
  * FR20 - Add one or more medications to the prescription.
  * FR21 - Record medicine name, dosage, frequency, and duration for each prescription item.
  * FR22 - Store finalized consultation into patient's permanent medical history.
  * FR23 - Cryptographic/lock guard: System locks consultation once completed (immutable).

Permitted Desktop Screens:
  1. Doctor Clinical Workstation (DoctorConsultationPanel)
     - Automatically locked to the logged-in doctor's identity (e.g., Dr. Muhammad Bilal).
     - Doctor selection dropdown is locked to prevent switching to other doctors' sessions.
     - Queue displays only patients booked for this doctor.
  2. My Scheduled Appointments (DoctorAppointmentsPanel / Read-Only Queue)
     - Displays this doctor's daily schedule, token numbers, and patient names.
     - Privacy protected: Doctor cannot inspect appointments belonging to other clinicians.

Privacy & Forbidden Actions (STRICTLY PROHIBITED):
  [X] CANNOT view appointments or consultation records of other doctors' patients.
  [X] CANNOT register new walk-in patients or edit general demographic records (Receptionist only).
  [X] CANNOT book front-desk appointments or cancel front-desk tokens (Receptionist only).
  [X] CANNOT access Billing Terminal, collect cash/card payments, or issue receipts (Cashier separation).
  [X] CANNOT register new doctors, modify working shifts, or alter consultation fees (Admin only).
  [X] CANNOT view clinic-wide revenue reports or financial totals (Admin only).
  [X] CANNOT modify or tamper with a completed consultation once finalized and locked (FR23).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 3: ADMINISTRATOR (Clinic Operations & Governance)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Authority Level:
  System Configuration, Staff Governance & Clinic Analytics Authority.
  Responsible for clinician onboarding, schedule configuration, and audit reports.

Allowed Functional Requirements (FRs):
  * FR6  - Register doctors with doctor ID, name, specialization, qualification, and fee.
  * FR7  - Assign each doctor to one clinical department.
  * FR8  - Define each doctor's working days (Monday - Sunday) and consultation time slots.
  * FR9  - Mark doctor as Available or Unavailable for any specified date.
  * FR10 - View and filter doctors by department and specialization.
  * FR27 - Generate daily and date-range appointment volume and status reports.
  * FR28 - Generate doctor-wise patient consultation and workload reports.
  * FR29 - Generate patient visit-history audit reports.
  * FR30 - Generate daily and date-range clinic revenue and financial reports.

Permitted Desktop Screens:
  1. Doctor Management & Scheduling (DoctorManagementPanel - Full Administrative Mode)
     - "Register Doctor", "Edit Profile", "Configure Working Days & Slots" fully enabled.
  2. Clinic Analytics & Revenue Reports (ReportsPanel)
     - Full access to appointment counts, doctor workload, visit history, and total revenue.
  3. Patient Directory Audit (PatientManagementPanel - Audit Mode)
     - Search patients and view registration history for compliance auditing.
  4. Clinic-Wide Appointment Queue (AppointmentManagementPanel - Audit Mode)
     - View clinic schedule overview across all departments.

Privacy & Forbidden Actions (STRICTLY PROHIBITED):
  [X] CANNOT conduct clinical examinations, diagnose patients, or record symptoms (Doctor only).
  [X] CANNOT write, modify, or sign electronic prescriptions (Doctor only).
  [X] CANNOT collect cashier payments or act as a front-desk cashier during clinic shifts.
  [X] CANNOT alter or delete completed consultation records (FR23 immutability is absolute).
